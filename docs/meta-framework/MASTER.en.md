# MASTER - Supreme Meta-Framework for Systems Engineering

> This is the MASTER document. Start here.
> This framework is the definitive orchestrator for generating, managing, and evolving high-complexity systems in any niche, market, or scale.

## 1. Framework Overview (71 Modules)

This system is a **Total Engineering Meta-Framework**. It covers the entire product lifecycle: from Discovery and business strategy, through mission-critical architecture, advanced cybersecurity, autonomous AI, to self-healing and systemic evolution.

**Unique Differentiator:** Designed to be read and executed by AIs (AI-First Documentation), with a focus on token efficiency, Zero Trust security, and financial sustainability (FinOps).

**Scope:** Capable of generating everything from quick MVPs to decentralized banking infrastructures or decades-old legacy system modernization.
